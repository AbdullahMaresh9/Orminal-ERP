    41→## **0.1 Global Standards Applied to Every Module**
    42→
    43→-   Every posted document is immutable; corrections use reversal,
    44→    return, amendment or adjustment.
    45→
    46→-   Every state transition is permission-aware and audit-logged.
    47→
    48→-   Every API write operation validates authentication, authorization,
    49→    idempotency where required, business rules and field constraints.
    50→
    51→-   Every screen exposes loading, empty, error and success states.
    52→
    53→-   Every report enforces the same data scope as screens and APIs.
    54→
    55→-   Every export is audited and subject to permission and
    56→    field-sensitivity rules.
    57→
    58→-   Every workflow emits domain events and notification events through
    59→    the platform services.

   157→  Accounting Integration              All transactions create journal

   169→                                      postings, period unlock, payment
   170→                                      execution, tax adjustment,

   178→                                      dimensions, approval thresholds.

   181→                                      deleted; corrections are reversal
   182→                                      or adjustment entries only. Closed
   183→                                      periods reject postings unless
   184→                                      reopened by controlled workflow.

   192→                                      entries in open periods, trace
   193→                                      every posting to source documents,
   194→                                      reverse with audit, and generate
   195→                                      financial reports by company,
   196→                                      branch, period and dimension.
